Frequently Asked Questions — Wheatgrass Powder Export

Whole Leaf Wheatgrass Powder is made by dehydrating and fine-milling the whole young blade of grass, providing high dietary fiber alongside chlorophyll. Wheatgrass Juice Powder is produced by first juicing the fresh grass and then spray-drying or freeze-drying the liquid juice, resulting in a highly concentrated, 100% water-soluble powder with zero fiber and maximum nutrient bio-availability.

Because gluten is strictly localized within the wheat grain kernel, pure wheatgrass leaf and juice powders are naturally gluten-free.
