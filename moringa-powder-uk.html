Why UK Nutraceutical Brands & Food Processors Import Moringa from IndiRoot Exims

The United Kingdom represents one of Europe's largest and most active markets for health supplements, functional foods, and clean-label botanical ingredients. Following Brexit, UK ingredient importers and brand owners require raw botanical materials that strictly comply with UK Retained Food Safety Regulations enforced by the Food Standards Agency (FSA) and Food Standards Scotland (FSS).

IndiRoot Exims provides a direct, traceable bulk supply of premium Moringa oleifera leaf powder from India's sun-enriched agricultural regions. Our controlled drying and mechanical milling protocols preserve the leaf's rich natural chlorophyll, vibrant green coloration, and delicate nutrient profile while keeping microbiological counts exceptionally low.

Every lot undergoes multi-stage screening at accredited third-party laboratories (such as Tentamus India Pvt Ltd) prior to shipment to ensure total compliance with Great Britain Maximum Residue Limits (GB MRLs) under UK Retained Regulation (EC) 396/2005 and heavy metal safety thresholds.

The UK Import Standard

We meet the strict technical standards demanded by UK quality assurance departments:

  • Zero Irradiation: 100% natural, heat-controlled drying preserves vital enzymes.
