Indian Chilli Powder Product Spectrum

Hot Red Chilli Powder

Milled from sun-dried, stemmed or stemless Red Chilly pods (Teja S17, Sanam S4, Wonder Hot). Engineered for sauce manufacturers, meat processors, and seasoning blenders who require bold heat paired with rich color.

  • Pungency Range: 25,000 SHU to 65,000+ SHU (High Capsaicin)
  • ASTA Color Value: 50 ASTA to 80 ASTA
  • Key Varieties: Teja S17, Sanam S4, S334
  • Primary Use: Hot sauces, spicy snacks, industrial spice mixes

Kashmiri Chilli Powder

Renowned globally for its intense, deep ruby-red natural color and exceptionally mild, non-pungent flavor profile. Essential for clean-label food coloring, ready-to-eat meals, soups, and gourmet culinary rubs.

  • Pungency Range: 8,000 SHU to 15,000 SHU (Mild Heat)
  • ASTA Color Value: 100 ASTA to 140+ ASTA (High Natural Pigment)
  • Key Varieties: Kashmiri Chillies / Byadgi Blend
  • Primary Use: Natural food coloration, mild curries, marinades

NABL Lab-Certified IPM EU Grade Chilli Powder

Exporting Red Chilli Powder to Europe and regulated international markets requires precise chemical compliance. Because ground chilli is a concentrated dried product, regulatory authorities evaluate pesticide residues under Article 20 of EC No. 396/2005, applying a dehydration factor of 10.

At IndiRoot Exims, our IPM EU Chilli Powder is processed in ISO 22000 & BRC-certified facilities. Tested by NABL-accredited laboratory Eureka Analytical Services (Report TR-26-EKA3-048735-01, Date: 09/09/2026), every lot complies with EU MRLs across all 160+ screened pesticide analytes.

Yes. We supply high pungency Teja S17/Sanam Red Chilli Powder (25,000-60,000+ SHU) as well as authentic mild Kashmiri Chilli Powder boasting high ASTA color (100-140+ ASTA).

What standard packaging is used for bulk exports?

Standard packing is 25 kg multi-wall woven PP bags or multi-layer paper sacks with heat-sealed double inner polyethylene liners to preserve color and volatile oils.
