Direct Neem Powder Supply for Sri Lankan Herbal & Agricultural Sectors

Sri Lanka's vibrant botanical sector—comprising herbal soap manufacturers, Ayurvedic personal care formulators, scalp treatment brands, and organic agriculture suppliers—requires high-purity, low-heavy-metal neem powder that complies with **Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI)** guidelines and **NMRA** regulatory requirements.

At IndiRoot Exims, we harvest fresh Indian neem leaves and process them under shade-drying conditions to protect native bio-flavonoids and active compounds. Our spray-dried extracts offer high water dispersibility for botanical sprays, while maintaining low moisture levels to withstand humid tropical shipping and storage without caking.

With direct ocean feeder vessel connections from West Coast Indian ports to **Port of Colombo** in just 3 to 5 days, we provide a reliable supply chain supported by full SAFTA trade documentation.
