NABL Lab-Certified Indian Coriander Powder for Global Buyers

Indian Coriander Powder (Coriandrum sativum) is world-renowned for its distinct sweet citrusy aroma, warm flavor profile, and essential volatile oil content (linalool). International food processors, curry powder formulators, and bakery ingredient packers require guaranteed chemical and biological purity to ensure smooth import clearance.

At IndiRoot Exims, our IPM EU Coriander Powder is processed in ISO 22000 & BRC-certified facilities from single-origin coriander seeds. Tested by NABL-accredited laboratory Eureka Analytical Services (Report Code: TR-26-EKA3-047570-01, Date: 31/08/2026), every batch is officially verified as fully compliant with EU Regulation (EC) 396/2005 across all 160+ screened pesticide analytes.

We offer flexible export terms: Ex-Factory, FOB (Mundra / Nhava Sheva), or CIF destination port with complete Certificate of Analysis (COA) dossiers.

Can you supply both Whole Coriander Seeds and Coriander Powder?

Yes. We supply clean machine-sorted Whole Coriander Seeds (Eagle, Badami, Scooter grades) as well as freshly milled fine Coriander Powder.

What standard packaging is used for bulk exports?

Standard packing is 25 kg multi-wall woven PP bags or kraft paper sacks with heat-sealed double inner polyethylene liners to preserve essential oils.
