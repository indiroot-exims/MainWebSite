NABL Lab-Certified Indian Turmeric Powder for Global Buyers

At IndiRoot Exims, our IPM Turmeric Powder is milled from single-origin, clean turmeric fingers. Tested by NABL-accredited laboratory Eureka Analytical Services, every export lot is certified to contain minimum 2.5% Curcumin, zero foreign matter, and zero detectable allergen contamination.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does your Turmeric Powder comply with European Union (EU) pesticide MRL standards?

Yes. All shipments are tested for 527 pesticide molecules via LC-MSMS and GC-MSMS methods. All analytes return Below Limit of Quantification (BLQ), fully complying with EU Regulation (EC) 396/2005.

What is the certified Curcumin content in your standard export grade?

Our IPM Turmeric Powder is certified for a minimum 2.5% Curcumin content (ASTA 18.0 method), with batch-specific lab assays provided upon shipment.

What standard packaging is used for bulk shipments?

Standard packing is 25 kg multi-wall woven PP bags with heat-sealed double polyethylene moisture barrier inner liners. Custom paper sacks or jumbo bags are available on request.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our baseline MOQ starts at 500 kg for trial orders and up to 18 Metric Tons (1x20ft FCL container) for full commercial shipments.
