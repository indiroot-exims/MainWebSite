Direct Garlic Powder Supply for Sri Lankan Food Processing

Sri Lanka's vibrant food industry—encompassing commercial spice millers, instant noodle manufacturers, savory snack producers, and sauce blenders—relies on high-pungency, low-moisture garlic powder that satisfies **Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI)** import regulations.

At IndiRoot Exims, we systematically dehydrate selected high-solid Indian garlic cloves using controlled hot-air processing technology. By capping moisture strictly under 5.0%, our free-flowing powder resists humid tropical conditions during ocean transit and warehouse storage, eliminating clumping issues in high-speed automated machinery.

With rapid 3 to 5 day feeder vessel connections from West Coast Indian ports to **Port of Colombo**, we ensure seamless inventory delivery for industrial processing plants across Western and Central Provinces.
