Why Polish Food Processors & Supplement Formulators Import Moringa from IndiRoot Exims

Poland has established itself as one of Central Europe's most dynamic hubs for food manufacturing, contract supplement packing, and herbal processing. Polish importers and manufacturing facilities require raw botanical materials that strictly meet the health and safety regulations enforced by the State Sanitary Inspection — GIS (Główny Inspektorat Sanitarny) — and European border authorities.

IndiRoot Exims provides a direct, traceable bulk supply of premium Moringa oleifera leaf powder from India's sun-enriched agricultural regions. Our controlled drying and mechanical milling protocols preserve the leaf's rich natural chlorophyll, vibrant green coloration, and delicate nutrient profile while keeping microbiological counts exceptionally low.

Every lot undergoes multi-stage screening at accredited third-party laboratories (such as Tentamus India Pvt Ltd) prior to shipment to ensure total compliance with European Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs) under Regulation (EC) 396/2005 and heavy metal caps under Regulation (EU) 2023/915.

The Polish Import Standard

We meet the strict technical standards demanded by Polish quality assurance departments:

  • Zero Irradiation: 100% natural, heat-controlled drying preserves vital enzymes.
