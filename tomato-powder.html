Strategic Wholesale Sourcing for Industrial Food & Beverage Processing

In high-capacity commercial food manufacturing, liquid tomato paste presents significant logistics challenges—including high freight weight, refrigeration costs, short opened shelf life, and batch-to-batch acidity variations.

At IndiRoot Exims, our processing infrastructure converts ripe, red, high-solid Indian tomatoes into concentrated, free-flowing Spray-Dried and Hot-Air Dehydrated Tomato Powders. Our advanced spray-drying process preserves natural Lycopene pigment, rich tomato solids, and tangy acidity while maintaining residual moisture under 5.0% to prevent clumping.

Whether you are formulating instant soup premixes in Dubai, scaling snack dusting lines in South Africa, reconstituting sauces in Kenya, or manufacturing ready-meal bouillons in Europe or North America, our shipments arrive backed by full lot traceability and safety clearance profiles.
