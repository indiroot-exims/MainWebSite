Direct Neem Powder Supply for Kenya's Agriculture & Cosmetic Sectors

Kenya is East Africa’s economic hub for commercial horticulture, floriculture, organic farming inputs, and herbal cosmetics. Agricultural formulators and personal care plants in Nairobi, Mombasa, and the Rift Valley require high-purity, low-heavy-metal **Neem Leaf Powder and Extract** that comply with **Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS)** rules and **PCPB** guidelines for organic crop protection.

At IndiRoot Exims, we harvest fresh Indian neem leaves and process them under shade-drying conditions to protect natural bio-flavonoids and active compounds. Our spray-dried extracts offer fast water dispersibility for botanical agricultural sprays, while maintaining low moisture levels to withstand ocean freight without clumping.

With direct ocean vessel routes from Nhava Sheva and Mundra ports to **Port of Mombasa** taking 10 to 14 days, we support East African buyers with consistent stock availability and complete Pre-Export Verification of Conformity (PVOC) documentation.

Kenya Customs Documentation

Every shipment exported to Kenya includes:

  • PVOC Certificate of Conformity (CoC): Inspection clearance for KEBS entry.
  • Phytosanitary Certificate: Issued by Govt of India Plant Quarantine authority.
  • Batch-Specific COA: Lab analysis for heavy metals (Pb, Cd, Hg, As) and micro limits.
  • Certificate of Origin (COO): Documented for Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) clearance.
