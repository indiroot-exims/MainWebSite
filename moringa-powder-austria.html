Frequently Asked Questions — Moringa Powder Export to Austria

1. Does your Moringa Powder comply with AGES regulatory frameworks in Austria?

Yes. Our export batches undergo laboratory testing validating compliance with Austrian Agency for Health and Food Safety (AGES) guidelines. We provide independent COAs detailing microbiological thresholds and EU heavy metal conformity.

2. How is bulk cargo routed to landlocked Austria from India?

As Austria is a landlocked market, we route shipments via major European maritime gateways, primarily Rotterdam or Hamburg. From there, cargo moves to Vienna, Graz, or Linz via intermodal rail or road freight.

3. Is your export-grade moringa powder treated with irradiation or ethylene oxide?

No. Our moringa powder is 100% natural and processed using clean, low-temperature dehydration. We do not apply irradiation, ETO (Ethylene Oxide), or chemical fumigants, in line with Austrian and EU organic and natural supplement standards.

4. What are your shipping configurations and container options for Austrian buyers?

We ship bulk orders starting at a minimum order quantity (MOQ) of 500 kg per variant. For larger accounts, we offer 20ft and 40ft Full Container Loads (FCL) on standard sea and land intermodal delivery terms.

5. What type of mesh sizes do you process for Austrian tea blending and supplement labs?

We offer coarser 80-120 mesh grades for herbal tea blending and finer, micro-milled grades up to 200 mesh for nutraceutical tableting and encapsulation.

6. What is the MOQ for moringa powder export to Austria?

Standard MOQ is 500 kg per order across our Natural, Organic, and Finely Milled variants.
