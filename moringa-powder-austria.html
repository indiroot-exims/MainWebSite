Yes. Our export batches undergo systematic laboratory testing validating compliance with Austrian Agency for Health and Food Safety (AGES) and Federal Office for Food Safety (BAES) guidelines. We provide independent COAs detailing microbiological thresholds and EU heavy metal conformity.

2. How is bulk cargo routed to landlocked Austria from India?

As Austria is a landlocked market, we route ocean shipments via major European maritime gateways—such as Rotterdam, Hamburg, or Southern European ports like Koper and Trieste. From these entry terminals, cargo moves directly to Vienna, Graz, Linz, or Innsbruck via intermodal rail and road transit.

3. Is your export-grade moringa powder treated with irradiation or ethylene oxide?

No. Our moringa powder is 100% natural and processed using clean, low-temperature dehydration. We do not apply irradiation, ETO (Ethylene Oxide), or chemical fumigants, fully complying with Austrian Bio and EU natural supplement standards.

5. What type of mesh sizes do you process for Austrian tea blending and supplement labs?

We offer coarser 80-120 mesh grades for herbal tea blending (Kräutertee) and finer, micro-milled grades up to 200 mesh for nutraceutical tableting and encapsulation.
