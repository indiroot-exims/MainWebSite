Compliant Allium Raw Material Supply for German & European Food Manufacturing

Germany represents the central manufacturing and distribution hub for spices and seasoning mixtures in the European Union. Food processors, sauce formulators, and organic brand packagers in Hamburg, Baden-Württemberg, and Bavaria enforce stringent quality control parameters regarding **Ethylene Oxide (ETO) residues**, **Pesticide MRLs under EC 396/2005**, and **microbiological safety**.

At IndiRoot Exims, our export production lines process selected high-solid Indian garlic cloves using controlled hot-air dehydration without chemical fumigation. Capping moisture levels strictly below 4.0% ensures a stable, free-flowing powder matrix that resists clumping in automated dosing and filling equipment.

With direct ocean liner shipments from Mundra and Nhava Sheva ports to **Port of Hamburg** and **Bremerhaven** in 22 to 28 days, every lot arrives supported by comprehensive laboratory documentation from ISO 17025 accredited testing facilities.
