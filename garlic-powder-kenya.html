Direct Garlic Powder Supply for Kenyan Food Processing & Repackaging

Kenya's rapidly expanding food manufacturing sector—centered around Nairobi, Mombasa, and Nakuru—demands high-pungency, low-moisture garlic powder for bouillons, spice mixes, extruded snacks, and canned sauces. To ensure smooth clearance through customs, importers require raw materials fully compliant with **KEBS (Kenya Bureau of Standards)** and **PVOC (Pre-Export Verification of Conformity)** frameworks.

At IndiRoot Exims, we systematically process selected high-solid Indian garlic cloves using controlled hot-air dehydration. Capping moisture strictly below 5.0% produces a free-flowing powder that withstands humid ocean shipping and equatorial storage, preventing caking in high-speed packing machinery.

With direct sea container routes from Mundra and Nhava Sheva ports to **Port of Mombasa** in just 10 to 14 days, we provide a reliable, cost-effective supply line for Kenyan buyers and transit markets across Uganda, Rwanda, and the East African Community.
