Global Wholesale Supplier of Indian Gooseberry (Emblica Officinalis) Powder

Revered for centuries as an essential pillar of Indian traditional medicine and herbal cosmetics, Amla (Emblica officinalis or Phyllanthus emblica) contains one of the highest natural concentrations of Vitamin C, bio-available tannins (emblicanin A and B), gallic acid, and ellagic acid found in nature.

At IndiRoot Exims, we procure wild-harvested and systematically cultivated fresh amla fruits from premier growing belts across Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh. The seed kernels are carefully removed, and the pure fruit pericarp pulp is processed into two distinct export grades: gentle Shade-Dried Amla Powder for active botanical efficacy, and uniform Hot Air–Oven Dried Amla Powder for large-scale industrial manufacturing.

Whether you require fine mesh powder for natural hair coloration, anti-aging cosmetic face formulations, health supplement capsules, or food beverage enrichment, our export batches pass international customs, pesticide, and heavy metal screening.

Frequently Asked Questions — Global Amla Export

What is the difference between Shade Dried and Hot Air Dried Amla Powder?

Shade Dried Amla Powder is dehydrated at ambient temperatures, retaining maximum Vitamin C, gallic acid, and light color for natural hair care and ayurveda. Hot Air–Oven Dried Amla Powder uses controlled heating for lower moisture (<6.0%) and low micro counts for commercial food and cosmetic manufacturing.

Is your Amla Powder suitable for cosmetic and hair care applications?

Yes. Our Shade Dried Amla Powder is widely used by international cosmetic and hair care brands as a natural conditioning agent, astringent, and key component in botanical hair dyes and face packs.

Every shipment includes a Certificate of Analysis (COA) specifying Vitamin C/tannin content, moisture %, mesh size, ICP-MS heavy metal limits, pesticide screening, and microbiological clearance.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and packaging?

Our baseline MOQ for sea freight starts at 500 kg for Shade Dried and 1,000 kg for Hot Air Dried variants. Standard packaging is 25 kg multi-ply paper sacks or fiber drums with inner heat-sealed poly liners.
