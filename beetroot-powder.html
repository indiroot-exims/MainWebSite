Frequently Asked Questions — Global Export

What is the difference between Spray-Dried and Dehydrated Beetroot Powder?

Dehydrated Beetroot Powder is milled from whole dried roots, retaining fiber and earthy notes ideal for bakery, seasonings, and plant-based meats. Spray-Dried Juice Powder is 100% water-soluble with zero sediment, engineered for sports pre-workouts, drinks, and dairy.

Can Beetroot Powder replace synthetic red colorants (E162 substitution)?

Yes. Rich in natural betalain pigments, our beetroot powder delivers a vibrant red hue, serving as a clean-label alternative to synthetic food dyes in candy, sauces, meats, and drinks.

Every shipment includes a Certificate of Analysis (COA) specifying betalain %, natural nitrate levels, moisture, ICP-MS heavy metals, pesticide residues, and microbiology reports.
