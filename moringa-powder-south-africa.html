Why South African & SADC Companies Source Moringa Powder from IndiRoot Exims

IndiRoot Exims is a dedicated bulk exporter of high-grade Moringa Leaf Powder from India to South Africa. We supply importers, regional distributors, food manufacturers, herbal supplement brands, and cosmetic formulators across Johannesburg, Durban, Cape Town, Pretoria, and Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha).

South Africa serves as the primary commercial hub for Southern Africa — many South African wholesale buyers re-distribute Indian moringa powder across the broader SADC region including Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe, Zambia, and Mozambique. Direct ocean shipping routes across the Indian Ocean connect Indian ports with Durban, ensuring cost-efficient freight and reliable transit.

Every export consignment is tested at accredited third-party laboratories (such as Tentamus India Pvt Ltd) to satisfy stringent quality guidelines. We supply complete documentation matching Department of Health (DOH) and NRCS requirements, including 500+ compound pesticide screening and heavy metal clearance.

Frequently Asked Questions — South Africa Export Delivery

Is Moringa Powder allowed for import into South Africa?

Yes. Moringa Powder is permitted for import into South Africa under DOH and NRCS guidelines. Full documentation is provided including COA, heavy metal panel, multi-residue pesticide screen, and microbiology clearance.

Is your Organic Moringa Powder USFDA and EU Organic certified?

Yes. USFDA and EU Organic certification for our Organic Moringa Leaf Powder is available on request, along with full lab test reports so your import documentation is ready before shipment arrival.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 500 kg per order. We accommodate mixed-grade shipments within the contract volume.

Which South African ports do you deliver to on CIF terms?

We deliver CIF shipments to major South African sea gateways including Durban Port (ZADUR), Cape Town (ZACPT), and Port Elizabeth (ZAPLZ) with full bill of lading and customs documentation.
