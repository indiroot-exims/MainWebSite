Why Kenyan & East African Companies Source Moringa Powder from IndiRoot Exims

IndiRoot Exims is a dedicated bulk exporter of high-grade Moringa Leaf Powder from India to Kenya. We supply importers, regional distributors, food manufacturers, herbal supplement brands, and cosmetic formulators across Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, and Eldoret.

Kenya serves as East Africa's central commercial gateway — many Kenyan wholesale buyers re-distribute Indian moringa powder across neighboring markets including Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and South Sudan. India's close maritime proximity across the Indian Ocean ensures highly competitive sea freight rates and short shipping transit times to Mombasa Port.

Every export consignment is tested at accredited third-party laboratories (such as Tentamus India Pvt Ltd) to meet international standards. We supply full documentation matching KEBS (Kenya Bureau of Standards) requirements, including multi-residue pesticide screening (500+ compounds) and heavy metal clearance.

The Kenya Import Standard

We satisfy technical requirements demanded by Kenyan port inspectors and QA teams:

  • Zero Irradiation: 100% natural, heat-controlled drying preserves vital enzymes.
  • Vibrant Green Color: Cold-milling prevents thermal degradation and dark discoloration.

Frequently Asked Questions — Kenya Export Delivery

Is Moringa Powder allowed for import into Kenya?

Yes. Moringa Powder is permitted for import into Kenya under KEBS guidelines. Full documentation is available on request including COA, heavy metal analysis, multi-residue pesticide report, and microbiology clearance.

Is your Organic Moringa Powder USFDA and EU Organic certified?

Yes. USFDA and EU Organic certification for our Organic Moringa Leaf Powder is available on request, along with full lab test reports so your import documentation is ready before shipment arrival.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 500 kg per order. We accommodate mixed-grade shipments within the contract volume.

Do you ship directly to Mombasa Port?

Yes. We deliver direct CIF sea freight shipments to Mombasa Port (KEMBA) with complete bill of lading and customs clearance documentation for inland transit to Nairobi, Kisumu, or neighboring East African nations.
