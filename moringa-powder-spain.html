Why Spanish Nutraceutical Brands & Food Processors Import Moringa from IndiRoot Exims

Spain represents a key Mediterranean center for high-quality food manufacturing, botanical extraction, and wellness product formulation. Spanish importers and manufacturing facilities require raw botanical materials that strictly satisfy food safety protocols monitored by the Spanish Agency for Food Safety and Nutrition — AESAN (Agencia Española de Seguridad Alimentaria y Nutrición) — and European customs authorities.

IndiRoot Exims provides a direct, traceable bulk supply of premium Moringa oleifera leaf powder from India's sun-enriched agricultural belts. Our controlled drying and mechanical milling protocols preserve the leaf's rich natural chlorophyll, vibrant green coloration, and delicate nutrient profile while keeping microbiological counts exceptionally low.

Every lot undergoes multi-stage screening at accredited third-party laboratories (such as Tentamus India Pvt Ltd) prior to shipment to ensure total compliance with European Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs) under Regulation (EC) 396/2005 and heavy metal caps under Regulation (EU) 2023/915.

The Spanish Import Standard
