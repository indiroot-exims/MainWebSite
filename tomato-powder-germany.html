Compliant Tomato Ingredients for German & European Food Manufacturing

Germany is the central manufacturing hub for dry soups, instant premixes, organic seasonings, and sauce formulations across the European Union. Food processors and ingredient blenders in Hamburg, Baden-Württemberg, and Bavaria enforce strict parameters regarding **Ethylene Oxide (ETO) residues**, **Pesticide MRLs under EC 396/2005**, and **microbiological limits**.

At IndiRoot Exims, our export processing units convert selected high-solid Indian tomatoes into free-flowing Spray-Dried and Hot-Air Dehydrated Tomato Powders without chemical fumigation. Capping moisture levels strictly below 4.5% ensures a stable powder matrix that prevents lump formation in automated European packaging lines.

With direct ocean liner shipping from Mundra and Nhava Sheva ports to **Port of Hamburg** and **Bremerhaven** in 22 to 28 days, every batch is delivered with full analytical documentation from ISO 17025 accredited laboratories.
