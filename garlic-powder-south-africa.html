Direct Garlic Powder Supply for South African Meat & Food Manufacturing

South Africa’s substantial meat processing and savory food sector—famous for boerewors, biltong marinades, braai rubs, potato chip dustings, and prepared gravies—requires high-pungency garlic powder that meets stringent **Department of Health Regulation R638** hygiene standards and microbiological controls.

At IndiRoot Exims, our production units process high-solid Indian garlic cloves using controlled hot-air dehydration. By maintaining moisture strictly below 5.0%, our free-flowing powder avoids lump formation during extended ocean voyages across the Indian Ocean and stores efficiently in Gauteng and coastal warehouses.

With direct ocean container routes from Nhava Sheva and Mundra ports to **Port of Durban** and **Cape Town** in 18 to 22 days, we offer reliable stock continuity for buyers in South Africa and regional SADC markets (Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe, and Zambia).
