Freeze-dried mango powder is produced from ripe Indian mango pulp, flash-frozen and vacuum-dried to maintain natural sugars, dietary fiber, and Vitamin A & C content — without the heat caramelization or flavor loss of conventional drying methods. IndiRoot Exims exports food-grade, 60-mesh freeze-dried mango powder to beverage formulators, confectionery producers, bakery brands, and nutraceutical manufacturers across the USA, Europe, UAE, and Kenya.
