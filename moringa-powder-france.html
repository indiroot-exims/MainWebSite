Direct Sourcing of Premium Moringa Powder for French Industry

IndiRoot Exims provides a direct, traceable B2B supply link connecting India's prime agricultural growing belts with France’s leading health, cosmetics, and organic food sectors. We specialize in contract manufacturing and high-volume wholesale export, delivering raw botanical ingredients directly to processing facilities in Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Le Havre, and across France.

Sourced from sustainably cultivated Moringa oleifera plantations in Southern India and Rajasthan, our leaves are harvest-selected, cold-milled, and packed under sterile conditions to retain peak chlorophyll, amino acid, and anti-oxidant levels.

Every lot undergoes multi-stage screening at accredited third-party laboratories (such as Tentamus India Pvt Ltd) prior to shipment to ensure total compliance with European Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs) under Regulation (EC) 396/2005 and heavy metal caps under Regulation (EU) 2023/915.
