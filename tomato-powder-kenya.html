Direct Tomato Powder Supply for Kenyan Food Manufacturing & Repackaging

Kenya's rapidly expanding food manufacturing sector—centered around Nairobi, Mombasa, and Nakuru—demands high-color, low-moisture tomato powder for bouillons, instant soup mixes, snack dustings, and reconstituted tomato pastes. To ensure smooth customs clearance, importers require raw materials compliant with **KEBS (Kenya Bureau of Standards)** and **PVOC (Pre-Export Verification of Conformity)** frameworks.

At IndiRoot Exims, we process selected Indian tomatoes into high-purity Spray-Dried and Hot-Air Dehydrated Tomato Powders. Maintaining moisture levels strictly under 5.0% produces a free-flowing powder that resists high humidity during ocean shipping and warehouse storage in East Africa.

With direct sea container routes from Mundra and Nhava Sheva ports to **Port of Mombasa** in 10 to 14 days, we provide a reliable, cost-effective supply line for Kenyan buyers and transit markets across Uganda, Rwanda, and the East African Community.
