Freeze-dried banana powder is produced from selected Indian bananas, flash-frozen and vacuum-sublimated to retain natural fruit sugars, dietary fiber, and potassium without caramelization or heat degradation. As a premier Indian exporter, IndiRoot Exims supplies food-grade, 60-mesh banana powder to baby food manufacturers, bakery formulators, beverage brands, and nutraceutical companies across the USA, Europe, UAE, and East Africa.
