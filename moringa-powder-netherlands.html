Why Dutch Ingredient Distributors & Re-Exporters Source Moringa from IndiRoot Exims

The Netherlands acts as the primary logistical gateway for botanical raw materials (grondstoffen) entering the European Union. Dutch B2B buyers and import houses trading through Weststad, Schiphol, or the Port of Rotterdam require absolute regulatory compliance to pass Dutch Food and Consumer Product Safety Authority (NVWA) inspections without delay. IndiRoot Exims delivers container-ready Natural, Certified Organic, and Fine Green Moringa Leaf Powder direct from India's sun-rich growing zones to Dutch warehouses.

By sourcing directly from primary Indian agricultural regions under strict quality assurance programs, Dutch wholesalers, superfood processors, and sports nutrition formulators secure batch-to-batch consistency and competitive pricing. Our processing protocols eliminate chemical residues, ensuring complete adherence to European Maximum Residue Limits (EU MRLs).

Every lot undergoes multi-stage screening at accredited third-party laboratories (such as Tentamus India Pvt Ltd) prior to shipment to ensure total compliance with European Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs) under Regulation (EC) 396/2005 and heavy metal caps under Regulation (EU) 2023/915.
