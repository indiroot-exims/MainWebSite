Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping and payment terms?

We supply on an Ex-Factory, FOB (Mundra / Nhava Sheva), or CIF destination port basis. Payment terms are 100% advance or confirmed LC for export orders.

Do you provide third-party quality certificates?

Yes. Third-party testing via accredited laboratories like SGS, Geo-Chem, or Spices Board can be provided per batch upon request.

What is the standard export packaging?

Products are typically packed in 25 Kg PP bags with inner poly liners to protect against ambient air humidity during ocean freight.

Can you supply private-label retail packaging?

Yes. We accommodate private label packaging in custom pouch sizes (100g, 200g, 500g, 1kg) for international grocery and retail buyers.
