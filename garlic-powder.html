Strategic Wholesale Sourcing for Industrial Food & Spice Networks

In high-capacity automated food manufacturing, ingredient consistency, controlled particle mesh sizes, and low residual moisture dictate operational success. Inferior garlic powders clump rapidly, lose volatile allicin pungency, and disrupt automated dosing machinery.

At IndiRoot Exims, our processing infrastructure systematically dehydrates fresh, high-solid Indian garlic cloves using controlled hot-air dehydration technology. By locking in natural aromatic compounds while capping moisture under 5.0%, we supply a free-flowing, anti-caking powder matrix that dissolves effortlessly into sauces, gravies, snack dustings, and seasoning blends.

Whether you are stocking high-capacity spice distributors in North America, scaling meat-processing lines in South Africa, or supplying base seasonings to European or Middle Eastern manufacturing plants, our shipments arrive backed by full lot traceability and safety clearance profiles.
