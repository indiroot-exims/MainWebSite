Direct Tomato Powder Supply for Sri Lankan Food Processing

Sri Lanka's food processing sector—including sauce blenders, instant noodle soup formulators, savory snack processors, and commercial spice millers—demands high-color, low-moisture tomato powder that satisfies **Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI)** import regulations.

At IndiRoot Exims, we process fresh red Indian tomatoes using advanced Spray-Drying and Hot-Air Dehydration systems. By keeping moisture levels strictly below 5.0%, our free-flowing powder resists humid tropical conditions during ocean transit and warehouse storage, preventing clumping issues in high-speed automated machinery.

With rapid 3 to 5 day feeder vessel connections from West Coast Indian ports to **Port of Colombo**, we ensure seamless inventory delivery for industrial processing plants across Western and Central Provinces.
