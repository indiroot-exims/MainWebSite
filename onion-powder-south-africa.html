Direct Allium Raw Material Supply for South African Food Processing

South Africa represents the primary industrial manufacturing hub for the Southern African Development Community (SADC). Commercial spice millers, meat processing plants (producing boerewors, processed meats, and marinades), and extruded snack factories in Gauteng (Johannesburg/Pretoria), KwaZulu-Natal (Durban), and Western Cape (Cape Town) depend on consistent dehydrated raw materials with controlled moisture levels.

At IndiRoot Exims, we systematically process selected high-solid Indian red and white onions using controlled hot-air dehydration. By maintaining moisture strictly below 5.0%, our free-flowing powder resists humidity in long ocean transit and coastal storage, preventing clumping in automated dosing machinery.

With regular ocean container vessel sailings from Nhava Sheva and Mundra ports to **Port of Durban** and **Port of Cape Town** in 18 to 22 days, we ensure a reliable supply chain backed by comprehensive food safety dossiers.
