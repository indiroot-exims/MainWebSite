Strategic Wholesale Sourcing for Industrial Food & Spice Networks

In high-capacity automated food manufacturing, ingredient consistency, controlled particle mesh sizes, and low residual moisture dictate operational success. Inferior onion powders clump rapidly, lose volatile pungency, and disrupt automated dosing machinery.

At IndiRoot Exims, our processing infrastructure systematically dehydrates fresh, high-solid Indian red and white onion crops using controlled hot-air dehydration technology. By locking in natural aromatic compounds while capping moisture under 5.0%, we supply a free-flowing, anti-caking powder matrix that dissolves effortlessly into sauces, gravies, snack dustings, and seasoning blends.
