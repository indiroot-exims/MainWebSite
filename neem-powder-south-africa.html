Direct Neem Powder Supply for South African Commercial Sectors

South Africa’s commercial personal care and organic agricultural markets—centered in Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, and the Western Cape—require high-purity, low-heavy-metal **Neem Leaf Powder and Extract** that meet **Act 36 of 1947** agricultural input rules and **SAHPRA** cosmetic safety guidelines.

At IndiRoot Exims, we harvest fresh Indian neem leaves and process them under shade-drying conditions to protect natural bio-flavonoids and active compounds. Our spray-dried extracts offer high water dispersibility for botanical sprays, while maintaining tight moisture caps to survive 18 to 22 days of Indian Ocean freight without caking or degradation.

With direct ocean container routes from Nhava Sheva and Mundra ports to **Port of Durban** and **Cape Town**, we provide solid stock stability for buyers in South Africa and regional SADC trade corridors (Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe, and Zambia).
