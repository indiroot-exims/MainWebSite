How It's Made

The Freeze-Drying Process

Every batch follows a strict sequence from raw material procurement to container loading.

01

Harvest

Sourced at peak ripeness for maximum natural flavor, aroma, and vivid color.

02

Flash Freezing

Rapidly frozen to lock in cellular structure before moisture extraction.

03

Vacuum Drying

Frozen moisture sublimates directly to vapor under vacuum with zero heat damage.

04

Milling / Cutting

Milled to ultra-fine powder or customized piece sizes according to buyer specifications.

05

QC & Packing

Tested in certified labs and packed in moisture-proof, export-grade packaging with COA.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does freeze-dried cost more than spray-dried or hot-air dried powder?

Freeze-drying is slower and more energy-intensive, but preserves natural color, flavor, and nutrients far better than heat-based drying.

What is the MOQ for freeze-dried products?

Typical MOQ starts from 500kg depending on processing volume. Contact us for specific item MOQs.

Do you have lab reports for every item?

Yes, 100% of our shipments come with Certificates of Analysis (COA) and SDS documentation.

Can you provide pricing?
