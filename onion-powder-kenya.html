Direct Allium Ingredient Supply for Kenya & East Africa

Kenya stands as East Africa's central manufacturing and commercial hub. Food processing companies in Nairobi, Mombasa, and Nakuru—producing dry curry mixes, pilau spice packs, seasoning cubes, and instant gravies—require consistent, low-moisture allium raw materials that satisfy **KEBS (Kenya Bureau of Standards)** import mandates.

At IndiRoot Exims, we systematically dehydrate fresh, high-solid Indian red and white onions using controlled hot-air processing technology. By capping moisture strictly under 5.0%, our free-flowing powder resists equatorial humidity during ocean transit and warehouse storage, eliminating clumping issues in high-speed automated packing machinery.

With direct ocean vessel connectivity from Nhava Sheva and Mundra ports to **Port of Mombasa** in 10 to 14 days, we ensure reliable inventory supply for food factories in Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, and the broader East African Community (EAC).
