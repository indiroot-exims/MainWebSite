Banana Powder Variants Available for Belgium

1. Raw Green Banana Powder

Milled from unripened green bananas and naturally abundant in RS2 resistant starch. Popular among Belgian nutraceutical brands, gut-health formulators, sports nutrition manufacturers, and clean-label gluten-free flour blenders across Flanders and Wallonia.

  • Drying method: Sun / air drying
  • Colour: Off-white to light cream
  • Shelf life: 12–15 months
  • MOQ: 500 kg
  • Applications: Gut health supplements, functional beverages, diabetic-friendly foods, sports nutrition, gluten-free baking
  • Documentation: COA, MSDS, pesticide & heavy metal reports, EU Organic certification (where requested)

2. Dehydrated Banana Powder

Dehydrated via controlled hot air from fully matured bananas, delivering authentic flavor and natural sweetness. Extensively specified in Belgian infant cereal processing, biscuit premixes, instant desserts, and snack filling formulations.

  • Drying method: Hot air dehydration
  • Colour: Light cream to pale yellow
  • Shelf life: 12 months
  • MOQ: 500 kg
  • Applications: Baby food & infant cereals, bakery premixes, confectionery fillings, snack coatings, porridge blends
  • Documentation: COA, MSDS, pesticide & heavy metal reports, EU Organic certification (where requested)

3. Spray Dried Banana Powder

Ultra-fine, instantized, free-flowing powder engineered through high-efficiency spray drying. Ideally suited for Belgium's premium chocolate manufacturers, dairy processors, instant beverage blenders, and ice cream producers who require rapid, clump-free solubility.

  • Drying method: Spray drying
  • Colour: Uniform light yellow
  • Shelf life: 12 months
  • MOQ: 500 kg
  • Applications: Chocolate fillings, dairy drinks, ice cream bases, instant beverage mixes, protein powders
  • Documentation: COA, MSDS, pesticide & heavy metal reports, EU Organic certification (where requested)

4. Freeze Dried Banana Powder

Premium freeze-dried banana powder processed at sub-zero temperatures to preserve original sensory notes, color, and nutritional integrity. Highly requested by Belgian artisan chocolatiers, premium cereal makers, and clean-label health food brands.

  • Drying method: Freeze drying (Lyophilization)
  • Colour: Natural bright cream
  • Shelf life: 12–18 months
  • MOQ: 500 kg
  • Applications: Artisanal chocolate & pralines, gourmet cereals, granola, high-end nutritional supplements, fruit snacks
  • Documentation: COA, MSDS, pesticide & heavy metal reports, EU Organic certification (where requested)
